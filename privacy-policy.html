AutoPin Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

AutoPin is an Android application that helps users organize local images and videos and publish them as Pins to their Pinterest account.

1. Information AutoPin Accesses

AutoPin may access images and videos that the user explicitly selects through Android's folder selection system. AutoPin does not access folders that the user has not authorized.

2. Pinterest Account Information

When the user chooses to connect Pinterest, AutoPin uses Pinterest's authorized API access to perform actions requested by the user. This may include accessing the user's Pinterest account information, boards, and permissions required to create Pins.

AutoPin does not request or store the user's Pinterest password.

3. Pinterest Authorization

Pinterest authentication is performed through Pinterest's authorization system. Users can disconnect AutoPin from their Pinterest account at any time through the appropriate Pinterest account settings or through AutoPin when that functionality is available.

4. Local Files

Images and videos selected for AutoPin are processed according to the user's instructions. AutoPin uses Android's storage access mechanisms to access the folder selected by the user.

5. Upload Queue

AutoPin may maintain a local upload queue containing information such as file names, content references, upload status, and timestamps. This information is used to prevent duplicate uploads and manage upload progress.

6. Data Sharing

AutoPin does not sell users' personal information. AutoPin does not share users' personal information with advertisers. Data is transmitted to Pinterest only when required to perform a Pinterest action requested or authorized by the user.

7. Data Security

AutoPin takes reasonable measures to protect locally stored application data and authentication information. Sensitive authentication information should be stored using appropriate Android security mechanisms.

8. Data Retention

Local AutoPin queue information remains on the user's device until the user removes it, clears application data, or uninstalls AutoPin. Pinterest-related data is handled according to Pinterest's applicable policies.

9. Children's Privacy

AutoPin is not intended for children under 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AutoPin's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or AutoPin, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with the AutoPin project.
